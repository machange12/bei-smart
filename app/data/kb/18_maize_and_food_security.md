# Maize and Food Security

Maize is Kenya's staple crop, and its price is one of the clearest signals of food security stress in the country. That is reflected directly in how AgriPulse's coverage came together: the arid-county retail markets of Wajir, Mandera, Marsabit, and Isiolo were not in the original market list, but were added once it became clear each had 180 to 240 months of maize retail records.

These turned out to produce some of the strongest forecasts in the whole system, several with test error under 7%. That is not a coincidence — WFP and KAMIS monitor these specific markets closely precisely because they are food-insecure, so the data collected there is dense and consistent in a way that reflects sustained monitoring effort.

Maize prices in these counties also run structurally higher than in production regions, driven by distance from supply and local climate. Watching maize prices here is one of the more direct ways this system connects a price number to the reality of food access in the parts of Kenya most exposed to it.
