# How a Consumer Might Use AgriPulse

A household buying maize or beans in an arid county — Marsabit, Wajir, Mandera, or Isiolo — can use AgriPulse's Alerts page to check whether today's price is a genuine spike or ordinary seasonal movement for that specific market, rather than judging it against prices seen elsewhere or on the news.

This distinction matters because these markets structurally run higher than the rest of the country. North Eastern's median maize price, 66.47 KES per kilogram, is nearly double Coast's 36.32 KES per kilogram, driven by distance from production areas rather than anything wrong with the market. AgriPulse's anomaly detector already accounts for this — it compares a market against its own history, not against a national average — so a price that looks high in absolute terms may still show as NORMAL, correctly.

The Map page is useful for a wider check: if several nearby markets show the same pattern, it is more likely a genuine regional shift than a one-off local issue.
