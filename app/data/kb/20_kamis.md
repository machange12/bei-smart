# Data Source: KAMIS

KAMIS, the Kenya Ministry of Agriculture's Market Information System, is the source that keeps AgriPulse's price history continuous from 2022 onward.

When WFP changed its data collection methodology in 2022 and retired its old city-level generic price series, a gap opened in the record. KAMIS fills that gap, because it already reports prices at the named-market, disaggregated level that WFP moved to — so the two sources line up in structure even though they come from different institutions.

Because KAMIS is a domestic government system rather than a humanitarian one, its market selection is not driven by the same food-insecurity focus as WFP's. Combining the two lets AgriPulse carry a market's price history across the 2022 transition without a break, but it also means the two halves of a series' history were collected by different organisations with different methods, which is worth keeping in mind when comparing prices from well before and well after 2022.
