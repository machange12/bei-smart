# Why Central and Western Kenya Are Not Covered

AgriPulse covers six of Kenya's eight regions. Central and Western Kenya are absent — not from one data source, but from all three underlying sources used to build this system.

The reason is what these sources are built to do. Price monitoring in Kenya, whether through WFP, KAMIS, or FAO GIEWS, is concentrated on food-insecure areas: places where price shocks translate directly into hunger risk, and where humanitarian and government agencies need early warning. Central and Western Kenya are both net food-producing regions. They grow more than they need and are comparatively food-secure, so they simply are not the priority for this kind of monitoring infrastructure.

This is a genuine gap in AgriPulse, not an oversight in how it was built. If you farm or trade in Central or Western Kenya, this system currently has nothing to tell you about your own market — the absence reflects where price-monitoring effort has historically gone, not a judgement that those regions do not matter.
