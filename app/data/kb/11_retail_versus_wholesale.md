# Retail Versus Wholesale Prices

AgriPulse tracks two different kinds of price, and they are not interchangeable. Wholesale prices are what traders pay in bulk at major market hubs — Nairobi, Nakuru, Mombasa, Kisumu, and Eldoret in this system. Retail prices are what an ordinary household pays at the point of sale, and are tracked mainly in smaller and arid-county markets: Isiolo, Kitui, Makueni, Meru, Mandera, Marsabit, and Wajir.

Of the 27 live forecast series, 14 are wholesale and 13 are retail. A wholesale price and a retail price for the same commodity can move quite differently, since transport, storage, and trader margins sit between them. Comparing a wholesale figure for maize in Nairobi against a retail figure for maize in Isiolo is not a like-for-like comparison of "the price of maize" — they answer different questions about different points in the supply chain.

When using AgriPulse, always check which price type a figure refers to before comparing it across markets.
