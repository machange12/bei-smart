# Retail Versus Wholesale Prices

AgriPulse tracks two different kinds of price, and they are not interchangeable. Wholesale prices are what traders pay in bulk at major market hubs — city and county-town markets like Nairobi, Eldoret, Kisumu, and Nakuru Town. Retail prices are what an ordinary household pays at the point of sale, and are tracked across the large majority of the 66 markets in the system, including most rural and arid-county markets.

A wholesale price and a retail price for the same commodity can move quite differently, since transport, storage, and trader margins sit between them. Comparing a wholesale figure for maize in Nairobi against a retail figure for maize in a rural market is not a like-for-like comparison of "the price of maize" — they answer different questions about different points in the supply chain.

When using AgriPulse, always check which price type a figure refers to before comparing it across markets.
