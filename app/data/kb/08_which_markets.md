# Which Markets Are Covered

AgriPulse forecasts about 80 markets across all eight of Kenya's regions — Nairobi, Central, Coast, Eastern, North Eastern, Nyanza, Rift Valley and Western — drawing mainly on the Ministry of Agriculture's KAMIS system and FEWS NET's Kenya-wide price collection rather than the narrower WFP-only set used originally. Eastern and Rift Valley have the most markets (around 20 each), followed by Central and Coast (around 10 each); Nyanza, Nairobi, North Eastern and Western have a handful each. Markets are grouped into these eight regions using their county.

Both wholesale and retail prices are tracked, depending on what each market actually reports. City hubs like Nairobi, Eldoret, and Kisumu carry wholesale series; smaller and arid-county markets mostly carry retail series, which is what a household actually pays.

This is a large expansion from the 12-market, six-region set AgriPulse started with. Central and Western Kenya — previously uncovered because the original WFP-only pipeline didn't reach them — are now represented through markets like Thika, Kerugoya, Karatina, and Nyeri (Central) and Butere, Mumias, and Mbale (Western). Refugee settlement markets such as Kakuma, Kalobeyei, Dadaab, Hagadera, Ifo and Dagahaley are deliberately excluded from both forecasts and alerts: prices there follow humanitarian distribution schedules, not ordinary market supply and demand, so a forecast for them would be misleading. The Forecast page narrows coverage further to only the markets with data current within the last six months, so not every modelled market necessarily appears in that page's selector at any given time.
