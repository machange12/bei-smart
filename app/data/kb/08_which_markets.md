# Which Markets Are Covered

AgriPulse forecasts 12 markets across six of Kenya's regions. Wholesale prices are tracked in five city markets: Nairobi, Nakuru, Mombasa, Kisumu, and Eldoret. Retail prices — what a household actually pays — are tracked in Isiolo, Kitui, Makueni, Meru, Mandera, Marsabit, and Wajir, plus a retail maize-meal series in Nairobi and a retail beans (dry) series in Nakuru.

The regions represented are Nairobi, Rift Valley, Coast, Nyanza, Eastern, and North Eastern. Central and Western Kenya are not covered by any of the three underlying data sources (see the separate note on why).

The arid-county retail markets — Wajir, Mandera, Marsabit, Isiolo — were not part of the original market list. They were added once it became clear they had 180 to 240 months of maize retail records each, and they now produce some of the strongest forecasts in the whole set. That is not a coincidence: these markets are monitored closely precisely because they sit in food-insecure areas, which is also why the underlying data exists in the first place.
