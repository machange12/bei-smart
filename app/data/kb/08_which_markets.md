# Which Markets Are Covered

AgriPulse forecasts 66 markets across all eight of Kenya's regions, drawing on FEWS NET's Kenya-wide price collection rather than the narrower WFP-only set used previously. Coverage by region: Eastern and Rift Valley are the largest (15 and 14 markets respectively), followed by Central (9), Coast (8), Nyanza and North Eastern (4 each), Western (3), and Nairobi (2).

Both wholesale and retail prices are tracked, depending on what each market actually reports. City hubs like Nairobi, Eldoret, and Kisumu carry wholesale series; smaller and arid-county markets mostly carry retail series, which is what a household actually pays.

This is a large expansion from the 12-market, six-region set AgriPulse started with. Central and Western Kenya — previously uncovered because the original WFP-only pipeline didn't reach them — are now represented through markets like Thika, Kerugoya, and Nyeri (Central) and Butere, Mumias, and Mbale (Western). The Forecast page narrows this down further to only the markets with data current within the last six months, so not every one of the 66 modelled markets necessarily appears in that page's selector at any given time.
