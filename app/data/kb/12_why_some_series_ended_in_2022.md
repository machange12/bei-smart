# Why Some Series Once Went Stale, and How That Was Fixed

Earlier versions of AgriPulse had a real problem: seventeen price series — mostly wholesale series in Nairobi, Nakuru, Mombasa, Kisumu, and Eldoret — stopped updating somewhere between 2019 and 2022, yet were still being shown as if they were current, confidently-labelled forecasts. The underlying cause was a 2022 change in WFP's data collection methodology, which moved away from the city-level generic staple prices AgriPulse originally relied on, toward named-market, disaggregated commodity varieties published elsewhere.

Rather than patch this with a second and third data source stitched together, AgriPulse was rebuilt on FEWS NET as its primary source. FEWS NET republishes the same underlying Kenyan Ministry of Agriculture price series WFP does — a direct comparison on overlapping months showed a correlation of 1.000 — but with far more complete and current coverage. Rebuilding on it meant the previously-stale series simply became live again, using the same underlying prices, just from a source that kept publishing them.

The result: the discontinued-series archive that once held 17 series and 204 forecast-actual pairs is now empty. That is treated as a good outcome, not a loss — it means every commodity-market series AgriPulse currently models has recent data, and nothing needs to be set aside as historical-only evidence. If a series does go stale again in the future, the same archive mechanism is still in place to catch it.
