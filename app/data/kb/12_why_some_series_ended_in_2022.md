# Why 17 Series Ended Between 2019 and 2022

Seventeen price series in AgriPulse's records stop somewhere between 2019 and 2022 — mostly wholesale series in Nairobi, Nakuru, Mombasa, Kisumu, and Eldoret, plus one retail series for Beans (dry) in Kitui. These were not markets that failed; they were discontinued by the data provider.

In 2022, WFP changed its data collection methodology, moving away from city-level generic staple prices toward named-market, disaggregated commodity varieties. The old series stopped being published under their original definitions. KAMIS, the Kenya Ministry of Agriculture's own market information system, has filled the resulting gap with named-market prices from 2022 onward.

Rather than discard the 17 old series, AgriPulse keeps them as a validation archive. Because their forecast period has already occurred, the forecasts made for them can be checked against what actually happened — 204 forecast-actual pairs, with a median error of 10.4%. They are not shown as current prices; they exist to demonstrate that the forecasting method holds up against real, verifiable outcomes.
