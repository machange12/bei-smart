# Data Source: WFP via HDX

The backbone of AgriPulse's price history comes from the World Food Programme, distributed through the Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX). This is the longest-running source in the system, with monthly market prices reaching back to 2006.

For most of that history, WFP recorded prices as city-level generic staples — a single figure for "maize" in a broad area, rather than a specific named market and variety. In 2022, WFP changed this methodology, moving to named-market, disaggregated commodity varieties. That change is why 17 of the historical series in this system stop around 2019 to 2022: they were defined under the old methodology and were not continued under the new one.

WFP data is collected primarily to support humanitarian and food-security monitoring, which is also why its geographic coverage concentrates on food-insecure areas rather than the whole country — a pattern that carries directly into which regions and markets AgriPulse can and cannot cover.
